## About the Event

"Tokyo OSS Party!!" is an event held in collaboration with the Tokyo Metropolitan Government to solve various regional issues in an open and enjoyable way using technology. This event serves as the Fukui satellite venue.

- **Organizers:** Tokyo Metropolitan Government
- **Administrative Office:** Bornrex Co., Ltd.
- **Fukui Venue Host:** [Code for FUKUI](https://code4fukui.github.io/)

### Key Features
- **Public-Private Collaboration:** Participants select from 10 themes proposed on the [Tokyo Metropolitan Government's Public-Private Co-creation Digital Platform](https://p2cc-digital.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/).
- **Open Source Development:** Teams develop solutions and publish their work as open-source software on GitHub.
- **Diverse Participants:** The event is open to junior high, high school, and technical college students, as well as the general public.

## How to Participate

1.  **Register:** Join the [Code for FUKUI Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/codeforfukui/shared_invite/zt-1g30n0mnr-g8y7eTAegytui4riQJ8d4A) to sign up. Registration is open until January 26, 2024, or until capacity is reached.
2.  **Review Themes:** Explore the 10 challenges on the [Tokyo P2CC-DP website](https://p2cc-digital.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/).
3.  **Attend:** Bring your laptop to the venue on the event day.

## Example Municipal Themes

This repository includes reference materials for challenges proposed by several municipalities.

- **Suginami Ward:** Promotion of the "Suginami Web Museum"
- **Nakano Ward:** Digitalization of public performance information
- **Chofu City:** Centralized dissemination of sports event information

*Images and details for these themes are available in the `imgs/` directory and on the event website.*
